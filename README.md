# Study Suite

**Spaced repetition, practice questions, and timed exams — from your own content.**

A self-contained study app that runs entirely in your web browser. There's nothing to install;
you sign in with a free account. The app holds no content of its own — you load **packs** (small files
of questions, flashcards, and topic guides) and it gives you focused ways to study them.

**▶ Use it now: [studysuite.app](https://studysuite.app)**

It's currently in **beta**.

---

## Features

### Study modes

- **Review** — spaced-repetition flashcards in seven interactive card types, including **Label** cards
  (a figure with its labels covered). Grade each one Again / Hard / Good / Easy and it's scheduled to
  come back across the following days. Tick **Review and Recycle** to bring the practice questions into the same
  schedule. Choose which topics to study, week by week — or a whole week in one tap with **All / None**.
- **Practice** — the question bank one question at a time. Submit to see the answer, the
  explanation and a link to the right topic guide. Questions you've never answered come first;
  ones you've answered before come last — the ones you got wrong ahead of the ones you got right —
  and **View prior answer** shows what you picked last time.
- **Exam** — a timed, scored mock test with answers hidden until you submit. It picks questions
  you haven't answered yet first, while keeping the weighting you chose.
- **Study rooms** — work through practice questions together, each on your own device. Start a room from
  the practice setup and share its 6-digit code. Everyone answers for themselves, anyone can reveal, and
  each person's answers go into their own history. Rooms can be named and can keep points, with a live
  leaderboard (no timer), and Past study rooms keeps who was there, your score and the final standings.
- **Topic Guides** — in-depth guides built into every pack, one per concept. Search their titles
  *and* their content, print them, and jump straight in from any answer's guide link. Every week
  ends with a one-page **cram sheet**. Most guides end with a quick **Check your understanding**: a few of that
  guide's questions and cards, a new mix each time. Some guides carry figures you can work with — step through
  levels, tap a structure to name it, quiz yourself, play heart sounds and watch a heartbeat or an ECG run, step by step — and you can **bookmark** the ones you come back to.
- **Pharmacology** — a drug index built from all your active packs: use, mechanism, dosing,
  cautions, adverse effects, monitoring and interactions for each drug, grouped by class, with
  links back to the week that teaches it. The **Pharmacology Index** adds a core summary for each drug
  and lists every week that teaches it.
- **Atlas** — drawn sections of an organ in every plane: move through the levels, tap a structure to
  name it, search for one, or switch the labels off and quiz yourself. **The Brain** comes with the
  neuro weeks, and guides and cards open it at the right level.

### Practice and exam tools

- **Set up before you start** — pick difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard — any mix) and topics for each
  mode. Exams also choose which questions (any, fresh first, or fresh only), how to weight them
  (by system, pack or topic), their length and the timer. Your choices are remembered.
- **Skip** a question without it counting as seen.
- **Flag** any question or card to come back to; flags stay until you clear them and follow you to
  your other devices. **Filter** the question map to show only unanswered, flagged, incorrect or
  correct questions.
- **Gaps** — your topics ranked worst first, from the questions you got wrong and the cards you keep
  forgetting. One tap starts a Practice set or a Review on a topic.
- **Highlights and notes** — select text in a topic guide, the Pharmacology Index, a question or a card to highlight it
  in one of four colours or add a note; figures and atlas slices take a note too. They sync to your other devices and
  survive pack updates. Practice and Review show them once you've answered (change it in **Settings → Highlights**);
  exams start clean, and you can keep an exam's highlights. **Highlights** on Home lists them all by week, with filters
  and search.
- **Come back later** — leave Practice or an exam part-way and pick up where you left off; the exam
  timer pauses while you're away. Start fresh instead, and what you'd answered is kept.
- **Past exams** — your last 20 exams, including unfinished ones, with scores, ready to review
  question by question.
- **Question history** can be cleared for one pack or everything, whenever you want a clean slate.

### Your library

- **Study packs for your course**, unlocked by your access code and organised by school, year and course —
  add one with a tap. Add more **access codes** any time: account menu → **Add an access code**, or
  **Settings → Access codes** if you don't use an account.
- Toggle each pack **on or off**; every mode uses only the packs that are on, so you can keep every
  week loaded and study just this one.
- Packs that name their **sources** (a lecture, a case, a module, a quiz) get a **Sources** button under their
  counts: switch any source off to set its material aside everywhere. An item stays as long as one of its
  sources is on; guides hide only the sections that are off. Questions and cards show their sources under
  the guide link.
- **Updates install themselves.** A newer version of a pack installs when you're not in the middle of a
  review, practice set or exam, and a new version of the app when you open Study Suite or come back to it —
  your progress carries over, and **what's new** says what changed. To update by hand instead, turn off
  **Settings → Updates → Update automatically**; then the library offers **Update** per pack or all at once,
  and the header **Update** button reloads for a new version of the app.
- The **Pharmacology Index** and the atlases keep themselves up to date: a new version is taken by itself, with no
  **Update** to tap, and **what's new** still says what changed.
- Switched-off packs can be **offloaded** to save space — they stay in your library with a download
  button and come back in one tap (automatically after a week off; change it in **Settings → Storage**).
- Load your own packs by dropping in a file.
- After the app updates, **What's new** shows what changed; tap the version number at the bottom of
  the home screen for every update.

### Accounts and sync

- A free account keeps your packs and progress **in sync** across your phone, tablet and computer.
- **End-to-end encrypted** — your data is locked on your device before it's uploaded, so nobody
  else can read it.
- A **recovery code**, password reset, email change and account deletion — all self-serve.
- If two devices change between syncs, their changes are **combined** — cards, question history,
  past exams, packs and settings — so there's nothing to choose.
- **Your devices** and **Sync activity** in the account menu show which devices sync and what sync did on each one.
  **Copy for troubleshooting** copies it all as text if you need to report a sync problem.

### Feedback

- **Report a problem** from any question, card or guide — or send an idea. Offline? It's sent when you're back.

### Private, offline, yours

- No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your study data is encrypted on your device before it's synced.
- The shared study packs are **encrypted** — only an access code unlocks them.
- Works **offline** once loaded, and installs to your home screen or dock on iPhone, iPad, Mac,
  Android and Windows.
- **Backup all** / **Restore backup** save and restore a file copy of everything.
- Two colour themes, **Navy** and **Warm**, each in light, dark or black — or following your
  device. Switch light, dark or auto from the button in the top bar.

## Bring your own content

The app is content-free by design, and making your own packs is encouraged. Download the
[**instruction set**](https://studysuite.app/Study_Suite_Authoring_Guide_latest.md) (from the home
screen, or that link), hand it to the AI assistant of your choice along with
your own notes, and it produces a pack you can load — questions, flashcards, and topic guides in
the right format. The packs you create from your own material are entirely yours.

## FAQ

### The basics — how it works today

<details>
<summary><strong>What is Study Suite?</strong></summary>

A study tool with three ways to study — **Review** (spaced-repetition flashcards), **Practice** (one question at a time with instant feedback) and **Exam** (timed, scored mock tests) — plus **Topic Guides** and a **Pharmacology** drug index to read from. The app itself is empty — you load **study packs** that hold the questions, cards and guides. You sign in with a free account, which keeps your progress encrypted and in sync between your devices. It's currently in beta.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is it really free?</strong></summary>

Yes. Study Suite is free while it's in beta, with no ads and no tracking; you just need a free account. If that ever changes, I'll let you know well before it does. If you want to chip in toward the costs of building it, there's an optional "Support development" link, but it's never required and unlocks nothing.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Where is my data stored? Can you see it?</strong></summary>

Everything — your packs, review progress, scores, and settings — is stored in your own browser, on your own device, and synced to your account. There's no analytics and no tracking cookies. Before anything is uploaded, it's end-to-end encrypted on your device — so I genuinely can't see what you study or how you're doing.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I back up my progress or move to another device?</strong></summary>

Use **Backup all** to save a file with everything in it, then **Restore backup** on your other device (or after clearing your browser). Or create a free account and turn on sync (below). It's worth backing up now and then, since your progress lives in your browser and clearing site data would wipe it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work offline?</strong></summary>

Yes, fully. The app is a single self-contained file. Once it's loaded in your browser — or installed to your home screen — it keeps working with no internet connection. Your packs and progress are right there on your device.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I add it to my phone or computer like a real app?</strong></summary>

Yes — use **Add to Home Screen** (the app walks you through it for your device). One thing to know: an installed copy may keep its **own separate storage** from the browser version (on iPhone and iPad it always does). Just sign in inside the installed app and everything syncs across.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I make my own study packs, or use my own notes?</strong></summary>

Absolutely — that's encouraged. Download the [**instruction set**](https://studysuite.app/Study_Suite_Authoring_Guide_latest.md) (also linked on the home screen) and hand it to the AI assistant of your choice; it walks the assistant through turning your own material into a pack you can load. Packs you make from your own content are **yours**. (The app, its design, and the pack format are mine, but the content you bring into it is entirely your own.)
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is any of this medical advice?</strong></summary>

No. Study Suite and its packs are **educational material for exam preparation only** — not medical advice, and never a basis for real decisions about real patients. It's also in beta, so content can contain errors or go out of date. Always check against your own curriculum and trusted sources.
</details>

### Studying

<details>
<summary><strong>Why can't I hear the low heart sounds?</strong></summary>

S3, S4 and the rumble of a narrowed mitral valve sit below what most phone and laptop speakers can play. Use earphones. Sounds are meant to play even with your phone's silent switch on.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How does Practice decide the order of questions?</strong></summary>

Questions you've never answered come first. Ones you've answered before go to the end — the ones you got wrong last time ahead of the ones you got right — and they're marked on the question map. Each starts clear, with **View prior answer** if you want to see what you picked before. Prefer only new ones? Turn on **Unanswered questions only** when you set up Practice. **Skip** moves on without counting a question as seen.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What is Review and Recycle?</strong></summary>

Spaced repetition for questions. On the Review deck, tick **Review and Recycle**. The practice questions in your active packs then come up alongside your cards, in the topics you've chosen, with the same Sources and Extras settings as Practice. After the answer shows, rate it Again, Hard, Good or Easy and it comes back on the same schedule as a card, except that Again brings it back tomorrow rather than in a few minutes. Questions you've answered in Practice start from that history, so the ones you got wrong come up first. New questions have their own daily limit in **Settings → Review**. An answer in Review also counts in your question history.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I leave an exam and come back to it?</strong></summary>

Yes. Leave part-way and the exam is saved, with the timer paused. Tap **Exam** to **Resume** — or **Start fresh**, which keeps the questions you'd answered (you can look back at them under **Past exams**) and sets up a new exam that avoids them.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What are study rooms?</strong></summary>

A study room lets up to 8 people work through the same practice questions at the same time, each on their own device. One person starts a room from the practice setup and gives the others its 6-digit code to type on Home. Everyone picks and submits their own answer. Anyone can press Reveal, and the answer, the explanation and everyone's picks show for the whole room. Your own answers go into your question history as usual. If you hadn't picked anything when the answer was revealed, nothing is recorded for that question. The question map lets you look back at questions the room has finished without moving the room, and anyone can end the room early to see the scores. Rooms can keep points: 100 for each right answer plus a bonus for right answers in a row, with a leaderboard after each reveal. Whoever starts the room can turn points off.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Who can see what in a study room?</strong></summary>

The others in the room see the nickname you chose and, once an answer is revealed, what you picked. While a room is open, my server passes those between you; it doesn't keep them after the room closes. Your own record of the room, including the final standings if the room kept points, is encrypted before it syncs, like the rest of your study data.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do highlights and notes work?</strong></summary>

Select text in a topic guide, the Pharmacology Index, a question or a card, then tap a colour or **Note**. Tap a highlight to change it or remove it. Figures and atlas slices have a note button of their own. Everything is listed under **Highlights** on Home, by week.

In Practice and Review your saved highlights appear once you've answered; the highlighter button shows or hides them. Exams start clean — tap **Keep my highlights** on the results screen to save the ones you made.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What are topics?</strong></summary>

Topics are the main subjects each week covers — anywhere from about 6 to 20 per week. They're what you choose from in **Review**, what you can narrow **Practice** and **Exam** to, and one way to weight an exam. Each question and card also carries a smaller label for its exact point.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What's the Pharmacology view?</strong></summary>

A drug index built from every pack you have switched on. Each drug gets its use, mechanism, dosing, cautions, adverse effects, monitoring and interactions, grouped by drug class, with links back to the week and topic guide that teach it.
</details>

### Accounts & sync

<details>
<summary><strong>What does sync do?</strong></summary>

Sign in on each of your devices — phone, laptop, tablet — and your packs and progress stay in step automatically. Changes sync in the background a few seconds after you make them, whenever you open or come back to the app, and every few minutes while it's open. Offline? Keep studying; it catches up as soon as you're back online. The cloud icon is **green** when you're in sync, **yellow** if the last sync is a few minutes old or you're offline, and **red** if something needs your attention. It's the automatic version of **Backup all**.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Do I have to make an account?</strong></summary>

Yes, since app v0.16.0. A free account keeps your progress safe if your browser clears its storage, and keeps your devices in step. If you were using Study Suite without one, nothing you did is lost: it's still saved in your browser, and it all moves into your account when you create one or sign in.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I create an account?</strong></summary>

From the welcome screen, choose **Create an account**. During early access you'll need your access code. You'll get a **recovery code** straight away — save it in a password manager or somewhere safe.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can you see my synced data?</strong></summary>

No. Sync is **end-to-end encrypted**: your data is scrambled on your device *before* it's uploaded, and only the scrambled version is stored. The key that unlocks it never leaves your devices. That means **I can't read your synced data** — not for support, not for anything — because I simply don't have the key.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What if I forget my password?</strong></summary>

Choose **Forgot password?** when signing in and you'll get an email with a link to set a new one. Then enter your **recovery code** to unlock your synced data — nothing is lost.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What if I lose both my password and my recovery code?</strong></summary>

Your **synced** copy can't be unlocked by anyone — including me. That's the whole point of end-to-end encryption. But **your studying isn't gone:** the data on your devices is untouched, and any **Backup all** file still works. Reset your password, choose **Start fresh**, and sign in on the device where you last studied — its data becomes your new account copy.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What if two devices change at the same time?</strong></summary>

They're combined — nothing to choose, and nothing studied is lost. If the same card or question was reviewed on both, the most recent review counts. Answers to the same question on both are all kept in your history. If a pack was switched on or off on both, the latest switch wins; a pack you removed stays removed. An unfinished exam that's replaced by a newer one moves to **Past exams**. **Sync activity** in the account menu lists what was combined, on any of your devices.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I see which devices are syncing?</strong></summary>

Account menu → **Your devices** lists each browser or installed app that syncs with your account, with its app version and when it last synced. Give the one you're on a name you'll recognise — names are saved inside your encrypted study data. **Sign out other devices** (under Security) signs out all the others at once.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What does an account store, and is it private?</strong></summary>

The minimum: your email address, your encrypted study data, a random ID for each device that syncs, and a few details needed to run the account. No selling data, no ads, no tracking. The [Privacy Policy](https://studysuite.app/privacy.html) lists everything; see also the [Terms](https://studysuite.app/terms.html).
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I delete my account?</strong></summary>

Account menu → **Delete account**. It's instant and permanent, and your data on your devices stays where it is.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I report a problem or send feedback?</strong></summary>

Tap the small ✋ button on a question, card or guide — or **Feedback** at the bottom of the page. It tells me which item you mean and sends a few diagnostic details (shown before you send). If you're offline, it goes as soon as you're back. I read every one.
</details>
